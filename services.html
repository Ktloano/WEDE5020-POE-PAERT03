Our Programmes - Food Scheme Services

1. Daily Feeding Programme - 200 Beneficiaries - Monday to Friday - Free

What: Hot cooked meal - pap, mogodu/ chicken, veggies, soup. Who: 120 school children (after school 2pm-4pm) + 80 elderly/unemployed (11am-2pm). How: Registered with ID and proof of residence. No payment. Prep: Starts 6am daily, 4 hours boiling, 50kg pap. Status: In stock daily, limited to registered. Cost to NGO: R30 per plate.

2. Monthly Food Parcels - 80 Elderly Families - R150 per Parcel - Category: parcel

What: 5kg mielie meal, 2kg beans, 1L oil, tinned fish x4, soup mix, fresh veggies. Who: Gogos who cannot walk to kitchen. When: Last Saturday of month, packing 9am-12pm. Delivery: Youth volunteers deliver within 10km Kagiso. Donate: R150 sponsors one family for month. Availability: 80 parcels max, waiting list 20.

3. Women Empowerment & Training - 10 Women Trained - Free Skills

What: Training in bulk cooking, hygiene, stock management, budgeting. Who: Unemployed women from Kagiso Ext 12. Duration: 3 months volunteer, certificate. Impact: 10 trained since 2022, 3 employed at schools. We Need: Trainers, kitchen equipment, aprons.
