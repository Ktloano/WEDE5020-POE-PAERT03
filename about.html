About Gogo's Kitchen Food Scheme NGO

Our History - From Gate to NGO

2018: Gogo Masego (67 years old) saw children in Kagiso Ext 12 coming from school hungry. She started cooking extra pap at her home gate. First day 5 children, second week 20 children. Neighbours brought vegetables. 2019: Local pastor donated pots. 2020 Covid: Fed 50 families during lockdown. 2021: Registered as NPO 123-456. 2022: Got small container kitchen from municipality. 2024: Now feeding 200+ per week with 5 volunteer gogos, 3 youth drivers. All work unpaid. Location: 123 Kagiso Ext 12, opposite primary school.

Our Mission

To ensure no child or elderly person in Kagiso sleeps hungry. To restore dignity through nutritious home-cooked meals, not handouts. To empower local women with cooking and management skills. Core values: Ubuntu, transparency, community ownership.

Our Team

  • Gogo Masego - Founder: 70 years old, 40 years cooking experience
  • Mama Nthabi - Head Cook: Volunteer since 2019, manages 50kg pap daily
  • Thabo - Youth Coordinator: Manages beneficiary registration and deliveries
  • 5 Volunteer Gogos + 10 Youth Volunteers

Where We Work

We serve Kagiso Ext 12, Ext 8, Tshepisong. Beneficiaries walk or are delivered to. We source meat from Kagiso Butchery, vegetables from Kagiso farmers market to support local economy. Open Monday-Friday 11am-3pm, Saturday food parcel packing 9am-12pm.
