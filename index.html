Gogo's Kitchen Food Scheme NGO - Feeding Kagiso Since 2018

Gogo Masego started in 2018 cooking pap and mogodu at her gate in Kagiso Ext 12 for 5 hungry children after school. Today Gogo's Kitchen Food Scheme is a registered Non-Profit feeding 200+ beneficiaries weekly - 120 children, 80 elderly and unemployed. We operate Monday-Friday 11am-3pm from 123 Kagiso Ext 12. No child sleeps hungry. We survive on donations, local farmers and volunteers. Registered NPO 123-456.

Our Impact - Real Community Work

Daily Feeding Programme - 200+ Beneficiaries - Free Meals - Category: feeding

Every weekday we cook 50kg pap, 20kg veggies, 10kg soup. Meals prepared by 5 volunteer gogos from 6am. Beneficiaries register with ID. Children get after-school meal, elderly get lunch. No fee, donation-based. In stock daily, preparation 4 hours.

Food Parcels for Elderly - Monthly Hamper - R150 - 80 Families

Monthly parcel: 5kg pap, 2kg beans, cooking oil, tinned fish, soup mix, vegetables from Kagiso farmers. Delivered to homes of 80 gogos who cannot walk to kitchen. Volunteer drivers needed.

Community Empowerment - 10 Local Women Trained - Volunteer Programme

We train unemployed women to cook, manage stock, basic hygiene. 10 women trained since 2022, 3 now employed. We need volunteers: cooking, packing, driving, fundraising. Hours: 7am-4pm.
